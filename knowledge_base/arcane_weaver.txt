Arcane Weaver

Arcane Weaver is the title held by the master of Lumis Academy, the global institution for sorcerous practice and dimensional defense. The title was held by Soren Vael, a formerly arrogant surgeon whose hands were destroyed in a high-speed vehicle collision. Pursuing restoration of his surgical capability, Vael traveled to Lumis Academy and encountered the Elder Sovereign, who initiated his training in manipulating dimensional energies.

Vael proved exceptionally gifted. He mastered techniques that took other students decades in a fraction of the time. After the Elder Sovereign's death at the hands of Nullis — the dimensional entity inhabiting the Null Dimension — Vael inherited the title of Arcane Weaver and became Lumis Academy's master and humanity's primary dimensional defender.

The Arcane Weaver's powers are derived from training in dimensional manipulation rather than biological or technological augmentation. He can project and shape dimensional energy into physical constructs — weapons, shields, conduits — create portals between locations at arbitrary distances, access alternate dimensions, and manipulate the flow of time within local constraints using the Chrono Eye, which houses the Chrono Core.

The Arcane Weaver is responsible for Nullis's containment. During an early confrontation, rather than destroy Nullis — which would have required the Arcane Weaver's death — Vael entered a time loop, repeating a single encounter with Nullis over fourteen million times until he discovered that the entity would withdraw in exchange for a deal. This demonstrated both Vael's extreme patience and his willingness to use unconventional solutions.

During the Nexus War, the Arcane Weaver gave up the Chrono Core to the Decimator to save Steel Titan's life, claiming he had foreseen exactly one winning scenario in over fourteen million examined futures. This sacrifice enabled events to proceed on the one path leading to the Decimator's ultimate defeat during the Final Stand.

Vael was erased after giving up the Core and restored by the counter-Erasure. He participated in the Final Stand and continues to operate as Arcane Weaver.
