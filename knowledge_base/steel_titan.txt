Steel Titan

Steel Titan is the armored identity adopted by Tane Voss, founder and former CEO of Voss Industries. Tane Voss is a brilliant engineer and industrialist who built a powered exoskeleton suit — known as the Titan Armor — after being captured during a weapons demonstration. Using his engineering genius and a miniaturized Pulse Core reactor embedded in his chest, Voss escaped captivity and later refined the Titan Armor into a sophisticated combat and utility platform.

The Titan Armor is constructed primarily from a proprietary titanium-gold alloy and incorporates the Pulse Core as its central power source. The suit provides flight via repulsor-based thrusters located in the boots and back, and offensive capability through pulse blasters mounted on the palms and an omni-pulse emitter in the chest. The onboard AI — named AXIOM — manages navigation, threat assessment, weapons targeting, and life support.

Over successive iterations, Voss upgraded the Titan Armor across more than fifty distinct variants. Each model improved on structural integrity, energy efficiency, weapons payload, and environmental sealing. Notable variants include the deep-space adaptation model, the suborbital entry model, and the Hulkbuster-equivalent heavy-assault frame colloquially called the Colossus Frame.

Tane Voss is a founding member of the Nexus Guard, the elite response team assembled by NEXUS Agency director Dax Frey. Voss contributed critical funding, logistics infrastructure, and technological resources to the organization. Despite initial reluctance to share proprietary technology, Voss eventually opened Voss Industries research divisions to collaborative Nexus Guard projects.

During the Nexus War, Steel Titan transported the Nexus Guard to the Decimator's forces in deep space. When the Erasure was executed, Voss survived and spent five years pursuing a reversal. He eventually provided the theoretical framework for the Chrono Raid that made the Final Stand possible. Voss personally wielded a replica Nexus Gauntlet bearing all six Nexus Cores and executed a counter-Erasure, restoring the lost population at the cost of catastrophic bodily trauma. He died from injuries shortly after.

Voss's legacy endures through AXIOM, through the Titan Armor schematics entrusted to NEXUS Agency, and through Voss Industries' continued development of clean energy technology based on Pulse Core principles.
