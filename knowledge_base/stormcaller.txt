Stormcaller

Stormcaller is the title and identity of the Stormhaven prince who wields the ancient storm weapon Stormhammer. Born of Allfather Vorn and the elder Sovereign of Stormhaven, Stormcaller possesses godlike physical power: superhuman strength capable of lifting thousands of tonnes, near-invulnerability to physical harm, and extreme longevity measured in centuries. His primary power is the command of storm forces — lightning, wind, and thunder — which he can channel through Stormhammer or project directly.

Stormhammer is an enchanted weapon forged at the Forge World by master craftsmen led by Elthor. Only those deemed worthy by an intrinsic enchantment may lift or wield it. When Stormcaller proves unworthy through arrogance, Allfather Vorn strips him of his power and casts him to the planet below, along with Stormhammer. Stormcaller must rediscover humility and selflessness before Stormhammer's enchantment releases and power returns to him.

On the planet below, Stormcaller encounters NEXUS Agency agents and eventually allies with the nascent Nexus Guard. He participates in defending Nova Harbor from an invasion orchestrated by his adopted brother Deceivus, who seeks to claim the throne of Stormhaven by engineering crises. The two brothers have an enduring complex relationship: Deceivus is a master of illusion and deception, a brilliant strategist whose ambition repeatedly brings him into conflict with Stormcaller, yet the bond between them never fully breaks.

Stormcaller later receives Arccleaver, a second weapon forged at the Forge World after Stormhammer is destroyed by Dusk Sovereign. Arccleaver is an axe-hammer hybrid that also channels storm forces. He uses it in the battle against the Decimator during the Nexus War. During the Final Stand, an earlier-timeline Stormcaller arrives and participates in the decisive engagement. In the aftermath, Stormcaller departs for the stars aboard a vessel assembled by the Cosmos Patrol.

The Arc Bridge is the dimensional transit mechanism of Stormhaven, connecting realms across space. Stormcaller can summon it or traverse it at will with Allfather Vorn's authorization.
