NEXUS Agency

NEXUS Agency is the primary covert intelligence and threat-response organization operating under international governmental mandate. Its full name is the Network for Extraordinary, Xenological, and Unconventional Security — an acronym that reflects its original founding charter following first contact with enhanced and non-human entities. The Agency operates across all nations, though formally subordinate to an oversight council of major state representatives.

The Agency's primary functions include intelligence collection on enhanced individuals and non-human entities, covert operations against criminal and insurgent organizations, administration of the Nexus Guard program, maintenance of classified archives on exotic threats, and deployment of field operative units for direct response.

Director Dax Frey led the Agency for the period encompassing the Nexus Guard's founding and the Nexus War. Frey is a veteran intelligence operative who lost his eye during an early field assignment. He typically wears a signature eye patch over the left orbital socket. Frey's leadership style emphasizes pragmatism, strategic deception, and long-term positioning — he withholds information routinely when he believes transparency would compromise outcomes, which generates recurring friction with the Nexus Guard members who prefer full disclosure.

The Vortex Syndicate conducted a major infiltration of NEXUS Agency, embedding operatives at high levels over decades. When this was revealed, a significant portion of the Agency's operational history was compromised, and many field operatives were placed at risk. This forced a complete restructuring of the Agency's personnel and operational protocols.

NEXUS Agency maintains a fleet of vehicles including aerial transport craft, submarines, and orbital platforms. Its primary Nova Harbor facility is an aircraft-carrier-scale flying fortress capable of vertical lift and sustained high-altitude flight — this vessel serves as mobile headquarters during field operations. The vessel was destroyed during the Vortex Syndicate infiltration crisis but subsequently rebuilt.

Following the Final Stand, NEXUS Agency undertook another restructuring to accommodate the new geopolitical landscape created by the Erasure and counter-Erasure events.
