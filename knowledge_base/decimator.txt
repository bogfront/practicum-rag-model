Decimator

Decimator is an ancient warlord of immense power who pursued a singular obsessive goal: the systematic reduction of all sentient life in the universe by exactly half. His philosophical justification was a cosmological resource theory — that finite universal resources would inevitably cause all civilizations to collapse through consumption, and that a controlled halving of population would delay or prevent this outcome. Critics and opponents universally rejected this reasoning as pseudo-scientific rationalization for mass murder.

Decimator's physical form is that of a massively built titan with purple-hued stone-like skin. He possesses superhuman strength exceeding most known entities, durability sufficient to withstand planetary-scale destructive forces, and tactical intelligence developed over centuries of conquest. He leads a vast military force — the Decimator's Army — organized around dedicated commanders and equipped with advanced weapons technology including energy-based armaments and orbital assault craft.

His ultimate instrument was the Nexus Gauntlet, a device of his commissioning forged by master craftsmen at Forge World under coercion. The Gauntlet provides a biological housing capable of concentrating the combined energy of all six Nexus Cores, allowing the wearer to exercise reality-scale effects across the entire universe instantaneously. Decimator spent decades assembling the six Cores — Force Core, Void Core, Flux Core, Psyche Core, Chrono Core, and Spirit Core — visiting each location and acquiring each Core through conquest or force.

Upon assembling the complete Nexus Gauntlet, Decimator executed the Erasure — a reality-scale elimination of exactly half of all living beings, selected at random, accomplished through a single activation gesture. The event took effect instantaneously across the universe, dissolving the selected individuals into fine particles.

Decimator was subsequently killed by Stormcaller wielding Arccleaver. However, the Nexus Cores had already been used and were destroyed by Decimator himself to prevent reversal. This forced the surviving Nexus Guard to pursue the Chrono Raid to reverse the Erasure.
