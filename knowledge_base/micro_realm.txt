Micro Realm

The Micro Realm is a subquantum dimensional layer accessible by shrinking beyond the subatomic scale using Varn Particles. The Micro Realm is not simply "small space" — it is a distinct dimensional environment with different physical laws governing space, time, and energy than those observed in the primary dimensional layer.

Entry to the Micro Realm is achieved by a Micro Warrior suit-equipped individual shrinking past the atomic scale and continuing to reduce until the dimensional boundary is crossed. Without precise navigation technology or an anchor to the primary dimension, the traveler risks becoming permanently lost. Harv Varn developed the navigation systems for controlled entry and exit. Scot Varn later advanced these significantly.

Within the Micro Realm, the traveler experiences time non-linearly. Different regions of the Micro Realm correspond to different temporal periods in the primary dimension — meaning that lateral navigation within the Micro Realm amounts to temporal navigation in the primary dimension upon re-entry. This was the key insight that made the Chrono Raid possible.

The quantum entanglement characteristics of Varn Particles in the Micro Realm preserve the physical integrity of anything brought through, including biological beings, indefinitely. This means the Micro Realm can serve as a storage environment for objects and people without subjective time passage or biological deterioration.

Scot Varn was accidentally stranded in the Micro Realm during the Erasure, which protected him from the Erasure's effect. He was later retrieved through a remote activation of his suit's exit sequence via technology left at Nexus Base. His detailed subjective experience of extended Micro Realm traversal — including the temporal anomalies he encountered — provided the research data Bren Valen required to design the Chrono Raid devices.

The Micro Realm's physical properties remain incompletely understood. NEXUS Agency research into it, led by Bren Valen following the Final Stand, is classified at the highest level.
