Crimson Wraith

Crimson Wraith is the operative identity of Nara Voss, one of the most accomplished covert agents in NEXUS Agency's history. Nara Voss was recruited into a clandestine training program as a child and underwent years of physical, psychological, and tactical conditioning that produced an agent with extraordinary hand-to-hand combat capability, proficiency in every mainstream ranged weapon system, mastery of infiltration and disguise, and deep expertise in psychological manipulation and information extraction.

Nara Voss carries a pair of electroshock batons capable of delivering targeted electrical charges for incapacitation or lethal application. She also carries a variety of concealed bladed instruments, compact firearm platforms, and specialist devices including micro-explosives and signal-jamming emitters. Her combat style integrates fluid striking sequences with environmental manipulation, making her highly effective in close-quarters and urban environments despite lacking superhuman physical enhancements.

Voss defected from the program that created her after recognizing the ideological corruption at its core. She joined NEXUS Agency and rose to become a trusted operative under Director Dax Frey. She became one of the founding members of the Nexus Guard and formed an especially close operational partnership with Crest Barlow, with whom she shared field assignments for years.

During the Nexus War, Crimson Wraith traveled with a strike team to a distant planetoid to retrieve the Spirit Core. She sacrificed her life at the Spirit Core retrieval site, which required an irreversible exchange — a life given willingly — to unlock the Core. Her death devastated the remaining Nexus Guard members. After the counter-Erasure in the Final Stand, those who had died to retrieve the Nexus Cores were not restored, as they had not been lost to the Erasure but had chosen to give their lives.

Nara Voss's sacrifice is commemorated at Nexus Base. Her operational archives remain classified at the highest level within NEXUS Agency.
