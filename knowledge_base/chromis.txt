Chromis

Chromis is a cybernetically enhanced former assassin, the adopted sister of Jade Striker, and one of the two daughters the Decimator adopted and trained as his personal operatives. Unlike Jade Striker, who defected from the Decimator's service early and joined the Cosmos Patrol, Chromis remained loyal to the Decimator for a much longer period, serving as a hunter and enforcer in his name.

Chromis's cybernetic augmentations are extensive, covering most of her body in mechanical replacement systems. The augmentations were not voluntary — they were imposed by the Decimator as both capability upgrades and control mechanisms, replacing organic tissue with machinery that could be adjusted, reprogrammed, or shut down remotely. Over time, she worked to reverse-engineer and disable many of the control systems while retaining the capability augmentations.

Her combat capability is extremely high: enhanced strength and speed from the mechanical systems, a neural interface providing direct electronic access to systems in her environment, and weapons integrated into her limbs. She also possesses tracking and analysis capabilities that made her an effective hunter of specific targets.

Chromis's trajectory toward heroism was more gradual than Jade Striker's and was accomplished partly through confrontation with her sister and partly through direct experience of the Decimator's indifference to her as anything other than a tool. During the Nexus War, she participated — as a past-timeline version pulled forward during the Chrono Raid's temporal complications — in the Final Stand on the side of the Nexus Guard.

The past-timeline Chromis who arrived during the Final Stand had not yet undergone the developments of the primary timeline's Chromis. Her relationship with the present-timeline events and with Jade Striker's past-timeline version created a complex interpersonal dynamic within the Cosmos Patrol's post-Final Stand operations.
