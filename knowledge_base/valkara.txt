Valkara

Valkara is a Stormhaven warrior of the ancient Valkyrion order — an elite corps historically tasked with selecting fallen warriors for transport to Stormhaven's warrior halls. She was the sole survivor of a direct confrontation with Dusk Sovereign that destroyed the rest of the Valkyrion order, and she spent centuries in self-imposed exile on a junk planet, working as a ship salvager and living in alcoholic disengagement from her past.

Stormcaller encountered Valkara on the junk planet when he was captured and sold to the Grand Arbiter — a flamboyant ruler who staged gladiatorial combat for entertainment. Valkara was the Grand Arbiter's champion handler and an effective combat operator despite her disengagement. After significant persuasion — including Stormcaller's appeal to her Stormhaven identity and her history as a Valkyrion warrior — she agreed to help Stormcaller escape.

Valkara's physical capabilities are at the elevated Stormhaven level: superhuman strength, durability, and longevity. She carries a pair of multi-function blade weapons and is among the most skilled warriors in Stormhaven's history, having trained and served in the Valkyrion order for centuries before its destruction. She also pilots spacecraft with considerable skill, developed during her years of salvage operations.

After the escape from the Grand Arbiter's world, Valkara returned to Stormhaven with Stormcaller and played a critical role in the Ruin's Edge event that destroyed Dusk Sovereign along with the realm. She led the evacuation fleet that saved the surviving Stormhaven population.

Valkara subsequently became a formal member of the extended Nexus Guard network, participated in the Chrono Raid, and joined the post-Final Stand Cosmos Patrol operations alongside Stormcaller. She leads the community of New Stormhaven's survivors in Stormcaller's frequent absences.
