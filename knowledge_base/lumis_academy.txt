Lumis Academy

Lumis Academy is the primary institution for the study and practice of dimensional sorcery, located in a concealed complex in Nepal. The Academy trains practitioners in the manipulation of dimensional energies — the forces underlying the structure of physical reality — and serves as the world's primary institutional defense against incursions from adjacent dimensions and entities.

The Academy was founded by the Elder Sovereign, an individual of exceptional longevity and power who maintained the role of supreme sorcerer for centuries by drawing life energy from the Null Dimension — a technique that extended her life but constituted drawing power from the very entity she was supposed to contain. This ethical compromise was revealed to her student Soren Vael, who was deeply troubled by it.

Students at Lumis Academy learn to harness dimensional energy by drawing on power from adjacent dimensions and directing it through training and will. The process is one of focus, channeling, and shaping — raw dimensional energy is everywhere, but using it requires extensive mental and physical conditioning. Graduates emerge capable of projecting energy constructs, creating dimensional portals (called sling rings in common usage among practitioners), and defending their local environment from dimensional intrusion.

The Academy maintains outpost sanctums in major population centers globally — one in Nova Harbor, one in London, and a third in Hong Kong — that serve both as operational bases for graduates and as anchor points for dimensional barriers protecting those cities.

The Chrono Eye, which housed the Chrono Core, was kept at the main Lumis Academy complex. The Eye served as both a containment vessel for the Core and as an amplification tool for time-related operations, allowing the master to observe possible future timelines. Arcane Weaver used this capability extensively, including his examination of over fourteen million possible futures during the Nexus War engagement with the Decimator.

After the Final Stand, Lumis Academy continues to operate under Arcane Weaver's stewardship.
