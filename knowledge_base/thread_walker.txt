Thread Walker

Thread Walker is the costumed identity of Piero Harken, a teenage student from the Nova Harbor borough of Midway. Harken was bitten by a genetically modified arachnid during a school field trip to Voss Industries' research division. The bite transmitted a mutagenic compound that rewrote portions of Harken's biology, granting him adhesive contact with any surface, proportional strength and speed far exceeding human norms, and a predictive danger-detection faculty called thread sense.

Harken also engineered a pair of mechanical thread launchers worn at the wrist, capable of producing a rapid-hardening polymer filament under pressure. He uses these launchers to traverse urban environments by swinging between structures, to bind and immobilize opponents, and to retrieve or manipulate objects at range. The launcher mechanism is his own invention, constructed from components sourced at minimal cost.

As Thread Walker, Harken operates in Nova Harbor's street-level criminal ecosystem before coming to the attention of Steel Titan. Tane Voss recruits Harken for the conflict known as the Nexus Schism, providing him with a sophisticated Voss-fabricated suit incorporating environmental sensors, an enhanced AXIOM-derived assistant named SAGE, and impact-absorbent layering. Harken is notably younger than the other Nexus Guard members and balances his heroic responsibilities with school, a part-time job, and relationships with his guardian Mira Janssen and his friend group.

During the Nexus War, Thread Walker joined the mission to Titan to confront the Decimator. After the Erasure, Harken was among those erased and therefore restored five years later. Following the Final Stand, he attended the memorial for Tane Voss.

Thread Walker's thread sense operates as a probabilistic field-awareness mechanism: it provides Harken with rapid involuntary alerts to incoming physical threats regardless of direction, giving him reaction speed that appears to exceed conscious cognition. Its exact mechanism is not fully understood even by Voss Industries researchers who examined Harken.
