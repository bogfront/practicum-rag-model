Deceivus

Deceivus is the adopted brother of Stormcaller and the biological son of the Frost Giants of Jotunheim — a realm whose population is in ancient enmity with Stormhaven. Adopted by Allfather Vorn as an infant following a battle with the Frost Giants, Deceivus was raised as a prince of Stormhaven without knowledge of his true heritage. He discovered his biological origins in adulthood, which compounded his long-held resentment about perceived favoritism of Stormcaller in the royal succession.

Deceivus's primary abilities are magical in nature: he is a supreme illusionist and shapeshifter, capable of projecting entirely convincing false images of himself and others, assuming any physical form, and creating persistent magical constructs. He also possesses enhanced Stormhaven-level physical capability and modest energy projection. His intelligence and capacity for long-term strategic deception make him a formidable adversary even against physically superior opponents.

His characteristic approach to conflict is indirect: he prefers to engineer situations in which others destroy each other, positioning himself to benefit from the resulting chaos. His relationship with straightforward physical confrontation is one of avoidance unless he has decisive advantage. He is a genuinely brilliant strategic mind.

Deceivus's relationship with Stormcaller is the dominant interpersonal dynamic of his existence — he simultaneously despises, envies, and loves his brother. Multiple times he has appeared to die or defect, only to reemerge. He has alternately fought against and alongside Stormcaller depending on whether their interests temporarily aligned or diverged.

He eventually died in genuine sacrifice during a conflict, interposing himself between Stormcaller and an overwhelming opponent. This death was not faked. Stormcaller acknowledged the loss as genuine and mourned him, though the Decimator subsequently used the reversal capability of the Nexus Gauntlet in a way that temporarily resurrected Deceivus — a distinction that matters because the restoration was mechanical rather than the sustained physical resurrection Deceivus would have required.
