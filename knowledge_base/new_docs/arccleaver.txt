Arccleaver

Arccleaver is the weapon forged for Stormcaller at the Forge World after the destruction of Stormhammer by Dusk Sovereign. Unlike Stormhammer — a traditional war hammer enchanted with a worthiness condition — Arccleaver is a hybrid axe-hammer of significantly greater destructive capability, designed specifically to defeat the Decimator.

The forging of Arccleaver required extreme conditions achievable only at Forge World: the dying neutron star's gravitational core was used as the forge itself. To sustain the required stellar temperatures during the forging process, Stormcaller was required to channel the star's raw energy output directly through his body for an extended period. The exertion nearly destroyed him — his body was pushed to the absolute limit of what a Stormhaven being can withstand.

Arccleaver is larger than Stormhammer and carries a greater capacity for storm force channeling. Its blade edge is capable of cutting through virtually any known material, and its blunt face delivers kinetic impacts amplified by storm force equivalent to Stormcaller's full power output. Unlike Stormhammer, Arccleaver carries no worthiness enchantment — it was forged purely as a weapon of last resort, not as a symbol of merit.

During the Nexus War, Stormcaller struck the Decimator with Arccleaver after the Erasure had already been executed, embedding the weapon in the Decimator's chest. Though the blow was fatal to the Decimator, it came too late to prevent the Erasure itself.

Arccleaver remained in Stormcaller's possession through the Final Stand. After the battle, Stormcaller departed with the Cosmos Patrol carrying both Arccleaver and the memory of Stormhammer.
