Iron Bulwark

Iron Bulwark is the armored identity of Jace Rhoen, a military officer and longtime friend of Tane Voss who operates a heavily weapons-integrated variant of the Titan Armor. Rhoen, a career military pilot and decorated combat veteran, was the first person outside Voss Industries to fly a Titan Armor variant — an unauthorized test flight that established the template for his eventual official role.

The Iron Bulwark suit differs from the Steel Titan Titan Armor in its military optimization: it prioritizes firepower payload over agility and carries a substantially heavier complement of conventional weapons. These include shoulder-mounted missile launchers, integrated minigun platforms, heavy-caliber rail weapon systems, and electronic warfare suites designed for battlefield operations. The suit is built to function within military command structures rather than independently, and its operational parameters reflect that orientation.

Rhoen's background creates a natural alignment with institutional authority that sometimes places him at odds with Nexus Guard members who resist external oversight. During the Nexus Schism, Rhoen supported the Kelvoria Accords and briefly clashed with Honor Guard's faction. However, his primary loyalty is to Voss personally and to the mission, and he navigates institutional and independent demands accordingly.

He participated in major Nexus Guard engagements from the Kelvoria crisis onward, including the Final Stand. He survived the Final Stand and continued operating as Iron Bulwark in the post-war period, serving as a liaison between the Nexus Guard and conventional military establishments globally.

The Iron Bulwark program is semi-officially recognized by the military establishment in a way that the Steel Titan program is not, given Rhoen's active commission status and chain-of-command integration. This gives the Nexus Guard a degree of institutional connectivity it would otherwise lack.
