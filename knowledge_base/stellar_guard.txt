Stellar Guard

Stellar Guard is the enhanced identity of Cara Davan, a former military pilot who was transformed by exposure to an experimental energy core of extraterrestrial origin. Davan was testing a light-speed capable aircraft built on technology provided by an alien scientist of the Krath species. When the aircraft's engine core was destroyed in an attack, the energy released was absorbed into Davan's cellular structure rather than killing her. She survived but was transported to deep space, where Krath civilization adopted her and she lived as a warrior for years, partially amnesiac regarding her original identity.

The transformation granted Davan extraordinary capabilities: the ability to project and absorb photonic and electromagnetic energy in concentrated blasts of enormous destructive power, flight under her own power in both atmosphere and vacuum, superhuman strength and durability, and the capacity to survive indefinitely in space without life support. At her full power output, she is considered among the most physically destructive individuals known to the Nexus Guard.

Davan returned to her home planet during a Krath conflict and recovered her memories. She allied with NEXUS Agency and became a member of the Nexus Guard. Her role is primarily as a heavy response asset for large-scale threats. She operates across interstellar ranges and is not permanently stationed at Nova Harbor or Nexus Base, preferring to remain mobile across the wider cosmos.

During the Nexus War, Davan was erased by the Erasure while in space. She was restored by the counter-Erasure during the Final Stand and arrived during the final battle to provide decisive firepower.

Davan carries a communication device modified to transmit across interstellar distances for summoning in critical situations. She had given this device to Director Dax Frey with instructions for use only in extreme emergency. The device's signal was broadcast by Frey in his final act before being erased.
