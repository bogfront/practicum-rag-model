Voss Industries

Voss Industries is a multinational technology and defense corporation founded by the Voss family and most prominently led by Tane Voss during the period corresponding to the Nexus Guard's operational history. The company's primary business lines include advanced defense systems, clean energy technology based on Pulse Core principles, aerospace engineering, computing and artificial intelligence research, and logistics.

The corporation was originally a major defense contractor, producing weapons systems for government clients globally. After Tane Voss took direct executive control following his father Vortan Voss's death, the company's strategic direction shifted significantly. Voss's experience during his capture — during which he witnessed the consequences of Voss Industries weapons used against civilian populations — prompted him to announce publicly that the corporation was immediately exiting the weapons manufacturing business and redirecting its engineering resources to clean energy and protective technology.

This decision was resisted by significant shareholders and caused short-term financial turbulence. Long-term, the Pulse Core energy program more than compensated financially while also transforming the company's public profile.

Voss Industries' research and development division — based at a Nova Harbor facility and at a secondary research campus — produced numerous technologies with significant applications across the Nexus Guard's operational history: the AXIOM AI system (and its descendants), the Titan Armor series, energy systems for Nexus Tower and Nexus Base, and various specialized equipment for individual Nexus Guard members.

Following Tane Voss's death in the Final Stand, control of Voss Industries passed to Pella Voss — his partner — and subsequently to a management structure she established. The corporation continues to operate across all its primary business lines. The Pulse Core energy division has expanded significantly in the years following the Final Stand, with Pulse Core–derived clean energy installations deployed globally. The company's AI research division operates under strict revised guidelines following the Oblitex incident.
