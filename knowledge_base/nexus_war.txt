Nexus War

The Nexus War was a universe-scale conflict precipitated by the Decimator's final campaign to collect all six Nexus Cores and execute the Erasure. It is the most destructive single event in recorded history, resulting in the death of approximately half of all living beings across the universe.

The war began with an assault on the Arc Bridge transit station, during which the Decimator's forces attacked a vessel carrying Stormcaller and Bren Valen. Decimator personally extracted the Void Core from Stormcaller's possession. Subsequent events unfolded across multiple theaters simultaneously as the Decimator's commanders pursued the remaining Cores.

In Nova Harbor, the Decimator's army launched a ground assault to extract the Psyche Core from Synthis. The Nexus Guard mounted a defense involving Steel Titan, Honor Guard, Sky Sentinel, Bolt Sentinel, Hex Weaver, Synthis, Arcane Weaver, and Thread Walker. Ground forces were defeated, but at enormous cost including Synthis's self-destruction to prevent Core extraction — a sacrifice that proved futile when Hex Weaver destroyed him and the Psyche Core was extracted anyway by Decimator personally.

A strike team including Steel Titan, Gamma Colossus, Crimson Wraith, Bolt Sentinel, Stormcaller, and Arcane Weaver traveled to the Decimator's stronghold planet Titan to intercept him before he achieved the complete set. Despite nearly succeeding in removing the Nexus Gauntlet, the plan failed when emotional intervention disrupted the extraction sequence.

The Decimator proceeded to the planet Auroria to obtain the final Core — the Spirit Core — via a different means, then acquired the Force Core from Vexor Corps. With all six Nexus Cores assembled in the Nexus Gauntlet, he performed the Erasure.

Half of all living beings across the universe were dissolved. Among those erased: Thread Walker, Stellar Guard, Bren Valen's field manifestation, Arcane Weaver, Jade Striker, Chromis, Gadget Ranger, Branchling, Tendrex, and billions of others. Survivors were left in shock. Stormcaller mortally wounded Decimator afterward with Arccleaver, but the Erasure had already been executed.
