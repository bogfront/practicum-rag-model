Cosmos Patrol

The Cosmos Patrol is a loosely organized crew of interstellar operatives who function as mercenary peacekeepers across the galaxy. The group formed from an unlikely convergence of individuals with varied criminal, military, and exile backgrounds who found common purpose in opposing large-scale threats to populated worlds.

The founding members of the Cosmos Patrol are: Pax Quell (Orbit Warden), a human raised among space pirates; Jade Striker, an assassin trained by the Decimator and sent to capture Quell; Gadget Ranger, a genetically engineered raccoon-like creature with mechanical aptitude; Branchling, a tree-like being and Gadget Ranger's loyal companion; and Granite Fury, a warrior from a destroyed world who misinterprets metaphorical language literally.

The group operates a salvage-and-transport vessel called the Milano (renamed the Benatar in later operations) and is most commonly hired through the Vexor Corps network, though they operate independently of any single organization's authority.

Pax Quell, as Orbit Warden, leads the group primarily through charisma and improvisation. He carries a pair of compact energy pistols and wears a pressurized flight mask for atmospheric and space traversal. He possesses a music player loaded with an extensive collection of historical recordings from his home planet, which serves as a significant personal anchor to his origins.

The Cosmos Patrol was instrumental in the defense of Vexor from Radion, a Krath commander acting under Decimator's authority. They later allied with the Nova Harbor–based Nexus Guard during the Nexus War, participating in the Titan mission and the Final Stand.

Jade Striker and Chromis — two sisters who were adopted by the Decimator as children and trained as assassins — both eventually defected. Jade Striker was lost during the Nexus War when the Decimator used her as the sacrifice price for the Spirit Core. A past-timeline version of Jade Striker arrived during the Final Stand and survived, creating a complex situation for her surviving sister.
