Micro Warrior

Micro Warrior is the suit identity adopted by Scot Varn, a former thief with engineering aptitude who was recruited — initially without full consent — by physicist Harv Varn to deploy a legacy suit. The Micro Warrior suit was originally developed by Harv Varn as a defense technology using his discovery of Varn Particles, a class of subatomic particles capable of reducing or enlarging the space between atoms, thereby shrinking or expanding any matter within a contained field without altering its mass.

The suit provides Scot Varn the ability to shrink to insect scale — roughly the size of a large ant — while retaining full human-scale mass and its associated kinetic properties. This makes him simultaneously nearly impossible to see or target while punching with the force of a full-sized human. The suit also enables growth beyond human scale to a giant form carrying the same mass effects. A communication interface in the helmet enables the wearer to signal and direct certain insect species, particularly ants of various types, who can serve as scouts, transport, and distraction.

Harv Varn's daughter Hope Vane — who had developed her own suit variant — is a co-operator of the Micro Warrior program. Her suit emphasizes speed, precision, and wing-enabled flight at small scale. The two operate as a partnered team.

During the Nexus War, Scot Varn was in the Micro Realm — deliberately trapped there for personal reasons — when the Erasure occurred, which shielded him from it. His experience in the Micro Realm following the Erasure provided the Nexus Guard with the key insight for the Chrono Raid: that the Micro Realm's temporal properties permitted past-timeline access.

Varn's contribution to the Chrono Raid was both as planner and as field operative, retrieving a Nexus Core from a historical location. His background in unconventional access — derived from his early career in theft — made him effective in extraction scenarios requiring stealth and technical improvisation.
