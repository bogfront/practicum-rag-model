Arc Bridge

The Arc Bridge is a dimensional transit mechanism used by the realm of Stormhaven to connect all Nine Realms — the inhabited dimensional spaces recognized in Stormhaven cosmology. The Arc Bridge appears as a massive rainbow-hued energy conduit when active, extending across dimensional space to connect two points regardless of physical distance. Transit through it is effectively instantaneous.

The Arc Bridge is anchored at the Stormhaven side by the gateway complex at the palace citadel, guarded by the gatekeeper Helm — an ancient being who has held the position for centuries and possesses eidetic awareness of all Nine Realms simultaneously. Helm can observe any location in any realm through the Arc Bridge connection and open or close dimensional gates on command from the throne.

The Bridge was destroyed by Stormcaller during an early crisis, when he smashed its mechanism to prevent enemy forces from reaching another realm. It was subsequently repaired and restored to operation. Later, during the evacuation of Stormhaven when the Cinder Sovereign event destroyed the realm, the Bridge was used for mass evacuation of the population.

Travel through the Arc Bridge without authorization from the gatekeeper is possible through covert means — Deceivus accessed it through dimensional pathways he discovered or created independently, and other practitioners with sufficient knowledge of dimensional navigation can approximate Arc Bridge-style transit. The Arcane Weaver and his students at Lumis Academy can create dimensional portals with functionally similar properties using sling rings, though without the throughput and reliability of the established Arc Bridge infrastructure.

The Arc Bridge's dimensional transit is not limited to physical distance — it connects genuinely separate dimensional layers, not merely distant points in the same dimensional space. This makes it qualitatively different from conventional space transit.
