Nexus Schism

The Nexus Schism was a period of profound internal division within the Nexus Guard, precipitated by the Kelvoria Accords controversy and culminating in a direct armed conflict between two factions of the team. It resulted in criminal charges against multiple members, the dispersal of the team, and a lasting fracture in the relationship between Steel Titan and Honor Guard.

The immediate trigger was the assassination of the Aurorian king — Tal Charo's father — at a UN signing ceremony for the Kelvoria Accords. The attack was falsely attributed to Frost Striker. Honor Guard, believing Frost Striker to be innocent, moved to prevent his capture. Steel Titan, aligned with the official investigative process, moved to facilitate it.

The deeper cause was the philosophical divide over the Accords themselves: who should control the Nexus Guard's deployment, and whether external oversight was compatible with effective operation.

The conflict culminated in a battle at an airfield facility where both factions clashed directly. The fight was damaging across the board — Steel Titan's Titan Armor was severely damaged, Honor Guard broke the Pulse Core unit in Steel Titan's chest in close combat, and both factions took casualties. Honor Guard's faction was formally declared criminal under the Accords framework.

The true orchestrator of the Kelvoria assassination — Baron Zevon, a Kelvorian nationalist seeking revenge for destruction of his country — was ultimately exposed. His manipulation of events revealed that the conflict between the Nexus Guard factions had been engineered. This did not, however, resolve the philosophical disagreement or the personal injuries sustained.

The Schism left lasting psychological damage to the team's social cohesion. Steel Titan and Honor Guard communicated minimally for the following years. The team was only fully reunited by the scale of the Nexus War threat, which overwhelmed the personal and political divisions.
