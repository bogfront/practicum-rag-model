Jade Striker

Jade Striker is one of two daughters adopted by the Decimator after the destruction of her homeworld — a mass extinction event the Decimator executed as a demonstration of his ideology. She and her sister Chromis were trained from childhood as elite assassins in the Decimator's service. Jade Striker was considered the most capable of the Decimator's personal operatives, feared across multiple star systems.

Her assignment to capture Pax Quell — who had stolen a valuable artifact the Decimator sought — was the beginning of her defection. After being captured by the Cosmos Patrol, she chose to remain with them rather than be returned to the Decimator's service. This decision was made partly from a growing recognition of the evil she served and partly from the personal connection she formed with Quell.

Jade Striker's fighting style is the most formalized and technically sophisticated among the Cosmos Patrol members, reflecting her childhood training under the Decimator's personal instruction. She is proficient in virtually every weapons platform and unarmed combat system she has encountered, with particular mastery of close-quarters blade work.

After the Decimator's death following the Nexus War, a resurgent Decimator from a past timeline — who used the Void Core to traverse time — targeted Jade Striker as his primary leverage point for compelling Chromis's cooperation. He sacrificed Jade Striker at the Spirit Core retrieval site to obtain the Core, using her as the required sacrifice payment. Her death devastated the Cosmos Patrol.

During the Final Stand, a past-timeline version of Jade Striker — who had traveled forward through the temporal bridge alongside the past-timeline Decimator's forces but chose to fight against rather than for him — participated in the battle and survived. This past-timeline Jade Striker does not carry the experiences of the primary timeline's version and represents a distinct person in an ongoing identity.
