Kelvoria Accords

The Kelvoria Accords is an international regulatory framework governing the operation of enhanced individuals — specifically the Nexus Guard — under collective governmental oversight. The Accords were named for the city of Kelvoria, which suffered significant collateral damage during the Oblitex engagement, a battle in which Nexus Guard actions — though ultimately saving the city from complete destruction — caused substantial civilian casualties and infrastructure damage.

The Accords' core provision is that the Nexus Guard may not deploy operationally without prior authorization from a UN-appointed oversight panel, except in cases of immediate active threat to civilian life where no time exists for authorization. Operatives who refuse to sign the Accords are required to cease field activities or face legal consequences under the international framework.

The political and philosophical debate over the Accords fractured the Nexus Guard. Steel Titan supported them, arguing that accountability was a necessary cost of operating with the degree of power the team wielded. Unchecked authority — even well-intentioned authority — posed systemic risks, and accepting external oversight was the responsible position. Honor Guard opposed them, arguing that the oversight structure would inevitably fail precisely when it was needed most, as political processes would introduce delays and compromises incompatible with effective response. He cited historical examples of external control producing worse outcomes.

Other members divided between these positions, and a direct physical confrontation between factions — during a mission to capture Frost Striker, formerly Honor Guard's closest partner — resulted in arrests, international criminal charges, and a deep fracturing of team cohesion.

The Accords remained formally in effect through the Nexus War period. During the Final Stand, practical necessity superseded legal framework as all available personnel responded regardless of Accords status. The post-war governance discussions around a restructured Nexus Guard continued without formal resolution of the Accords' applicability.
