Nexus Gauntlet

The Nexus Gauntlet is a purpose-built containment and concentration device designed to allow a biological being to safely channel the combined energies of all six Nexus Cores simultaneously. The Gauntlet takes the form of a large armored glove covering the hand and forearm, fitted with six housing slots corresponding to each of the six Nexus Cores. When all six Cores are seated and activated, the Gauntlet enables the wearer to exercise effects of universal scale through gesture.

The Gauntlet was commissioned by the Decimator and constructed at Forge World by master craftsmen under coercion. The craftsman Elthor was forced to create the device under threat to his people. The Forge World facility is the only known location with the technical capability to produce a device of this complexity, as the forging process requires stellar-core-level heat achievable only in proximity to a dying star.

Without the Nexus Gauntlet or an equivalent device, attempting to channel multiple Nexus Cores simultaneously would be immediately fatal to any biological being — the energy output exceeds what organic tissue can survive. The Gauntlet's design distributes and mediates this energy, though even with the Gauntlet, the physical cost of activation is extreme. The Decimator's own organic tissue was severely damaged by the Erasure activation. Bren Valen suffered destruction of his right arm performing the counter-Erasure. Tane Voss was killed by the Final Stand activation.

A second Nexus Gauntlet was constructed by Eithr during the Chrono Raid preparation period, using specifications derived from the original. This replica was used by Bren Valen for the counter-Erasure. Tane Voss incorporated a third configuration into the Titan Armor itself for the Final Stand, integrating the Nexus Cores directly into the suit's palm.

The Nexus Gauntlet's design has no known equivalent in any other technological or mystical tradition. Its core engineering involves exotic matter physics beyond the current global understanding of materials science.
