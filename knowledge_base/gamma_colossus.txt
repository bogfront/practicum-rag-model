Gamma Colossus

Gamma Colossus is the alternate form of physicist Bren Valen, triggered by intense emotional arousal or physical threat. Valen was exposed to a massive dose of gamma radiation during a weapons experiment he was conducting. Rather than killing him, the radiation fused with a latent genetic factor in his biology, creating a dual-state existence. In his default human form, Valen is a brilliant scientist specializing in gamma radiation, quantum physics, and bio-energetics. When sufficiently provoked, he undergoes rapid involuntary transformation into the Gamma Colossus: a massive, enormously powerful green-skinned entity with strength that scales with emotional intensity.

The Gamma Colossus possesses near-limitless physical strength, extraordinary durability, and accelerated regeneration. He can survive vacuum, withstand nuclear-level explosive forces, and recover from wounds that would be fatal to any other being. His emotional state directly amplifies his power — the angrier the Colossus, the stronger he becomes. In early manifestations, the Gamma Colossus was nearly uncontrollable and regarded as a threat by NEXUS Agency. Bren Valen spent years in hiding, attempting to suppress the transformations.

Valen eventually joined the Nexus Guard after Director Dax Frey's personal outreach. He participated in defending Nova Harbor during the battle against Deceivus's forces and proved the Gamma Colossus could be directed, though imperfectly. Over years of work with the team, Valen developed methods to maintain intellectual continuity during transformation, eventually achieving a merged state combining his scientific mind with the Colossus's physical form — referred to as the Rational Colossus or simply Valen-Prime.

Valen-Prime performed the initial counter-Erasure using a replica Nexus Gauntlet during the Chrono Raid, though the exertion severely damaged his right arm. The limb was later partially restored through a combination of aurite-weave prosthetic augmentation and regenerative treatment.

Valen's research into gamma radiation, conducted both at NEXUS Agency facilities and independently, produced numerous papers referenced in the fields of high-energy biology and mutagenic physics.
