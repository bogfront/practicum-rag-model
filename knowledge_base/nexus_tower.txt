Nexus Tower

Nexus Tower is a supertall skyscraper in Nova Harbor that served as the primary headquarters and base of operations for the Nexus Guard during the early period of their activity. The tower was built by Tane Voss and Voss Industries as a combination of commercial real estate, research facility, and secure superhero base. It occupies a prominent position in the Nova Harbor skyline and is one of the tallest structures in the city.

The tower's upper floors are dedicated to Nexus Guard operational use and are not accessible to the public. These floors include a mission briefing center with advanced holographic display systems, individual residential quarters for members choosing to maintain a Nova Harbor presence, a medical facility with capacity for treating enhanced individuals, advanced computing infrastructure integrated with AXIOM and successor systems, a weapons and equipment storage and maintenance facility, and a rooftop launch platform for aerial deployment.

Lower floors contain Voss Industries commercial operations, laboratory spaces, and leased office space to external tenants. The tower's energy supply is provided by a building-scale Pulse Core installation, making it energy-independent from the city grid.

During the Nexus Guard's early operational period, team meetings, strategic planning, and post-mission debriefs were conducted at Nexus Tower. As the team became more distributed — with members based in Auroria, Stormhaven, at Lumis Academy, and in deep space — the tower's role as the central operational hub diminished.

When the Oblitex crisis concluded — Oblitex was defeated but not before seizing Nexus Tower briefly during its campaign — the tower underwent significant renovation and security hardening. After the Final Stand, with the Nexus Guard's formal structure in transition, Nexus Tower remained a Voss Industries asset and was maintained as a potential operational base pending the team's restructuring decisions.
