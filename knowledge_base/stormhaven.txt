Stormhaven

Stormhaven is a realm of advanced civilization existing in a separate dimensional space connected to the planet below by the Arc Bridge, a dimensional transit mechanism. Stormhaven's inhabitants are long-lived beings of great physical power, culturally structured around warrior traditions, governance by a royal monarchy, and stewardship of several connected realms — collectively called the Nine Realms.

The ruling structure of Stormhaven centers on the royal lineage. Allfather Vorn held the throne for centuries before passing it to Stormcaller. The royal line possesses artifacts of concentrated power, including the Void Prism which housed the Void Core, and the historical weapon collections maintained in the vaults beneath the palace.

Stormhaven's greatest historical enemy is Nullis, the entity inhabiting the Null Dimension, who has attacked Stormhaven multiple times across the centuries. Other major threats include Dusk Sovereign, the firstborn child of Allfather Vorn, who was imprisoned for millennia following an attempt to conquer the Nine Realms. She was released when Allfather Vorn died and rapidly demonstrated power exceeding Stormcaller's, destroying Stormhammer in direct confrontation.

To defeat Dusk Sovereign without suicidal direct confrontation, Stormcaller triggered a prophesied destructive event called the Ruin's Edge — a catastrophic fire-giant assault on Stormhaven orchestrated by Cinder Sovereign, a legendary being imprisoned beneath the planet. The resulting conflagration destroyed Stormhaven itself, killing Dusk Sovereign in the process. The surviving population evacuated on a salvaged vessel.

After the Erasure, roughly half of Stormhaven's surviving population was lost. The survivors, including Stormcaller, worked with the Cosmos Patrol and eventually established a new home. A restored New Stormhaven was later established on territory granted by one of the planet's smaller nations in recognition of the Nexus Guard's contribution to the counter-Erasure.
