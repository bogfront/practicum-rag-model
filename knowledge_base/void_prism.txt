Void Prism

The Void Prism is a crystalline artifact of ancient construction that served as the containment vessel for the Void Core — the blue Nexus Core governing space and dimensional traversal. The Prism's form is a translucent cube-like structure approximately 30 centimeters per side, emanating a soft blue luminescence when the Void Core within is active.

The Void Prism was held by Stormhaven under Allfather Vorn's authority for centuries after it was discovered during an early Nine Realms conflict. Vorn recognized its nature and power and maintained it in a secure vault beneath the Stormhaven palace. It was one of the most closely guarded artifacts in the Nine Realms.

The Void Core within the Prism powered the Arc Bridge's dimensional transit capability and was referenced in historical records as the source of Stormhaven's ability to connect the Nine Realms. Whether the Arc Bridge's original construction required the Void Core or merely benefited from its presence was a matter of historical debate among Stormhaven scholars.

During an encounter between Decimator's forces and a vessel transporting Stormcaller and Bren Valen, the Void Prism was destroyed and the Void Core extracted by Decimator personally. This was the Decimator's first Core acquisition in the period of the Nexus War.

A historical version of the Void Prism — and the Void Core within it — was retrieved from Stormhaven at an earlier timepoint during the Chrono Raid. The retrieval team — Honor Guard and Bren Valen — encountered complications during the historical period they entered, as the timepoint chosen intersected with the period of the original Nexus Guard formation. Honor Guard briefly interacted with his own historical self. The Void Core was successfully retrieved despite these complications.
