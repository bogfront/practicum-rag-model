Forge World

Forge World is a realm of artificers and craftsmen who possess the most advanced metallurgical and weapons-forging capability in the Nine Realms. Located in proximity to a dying star — a neutron star — whose core provides the extreme stellar temperatures required for working with the most powerful materials and enchantments, Forge World is the source of the most significant weapons in Stormhaven mythology and history.

The master craftsman of Forge World during the contemporary era is Elthor, a survivor of a mass atrocity visited on Forge World by the Decimator. The Decimator forced Elthor to complete the Nexus Gauntlet under threat to his surviving people. Elthor complied but attempted to sabotage the project by constructing the Gauntlet in a way that would overload and kill the wearer — a plan that was partially successful, as the Erasure activation did cause catastrophic physical damage to the Decimator.

The weapons produced at Forge World include: Stormhammer — forged for a Stormhaven king, enchanted with the worthiness condition; Arccleaver — a later axe-hammer commission, forged using the neutron star's gravitational core as a forge, requiring Stormcaller to channel the star's energy while the forging proceeded, which nearly destroyed him; and the Nexus Gauntlet — commissioned under duress by the Decimator for Core concentration.

The forging process at Forge World involves channeling stellar energy — the star's dying output — into the material being worked. This process requires both extraordinary technical skill and the capacity to withstand near-stellar energy exposure during the working period. Conventional beings cannot survive the environment; Stormhaven beings can withstand it but only at significant physical cost.

After the Decimator's assault on Forge World's population, Elthor was the sole survivor at the main forge complex. The Nexus Guard expedition that came to commission Arccleaver found him alone among the ruins of his workshop.
