Battle of Nova Harbor

The Battle of Nova Harbor was the first major collective action by the Nexus Guard, fought against an invasion force deployed by Deceivus using a dimensional portal to bring a Krath military contingent to the planet. The battle established the Nexus Guard as a coherent operational unit and demonstrated the viability of Director Dax Frey's concept of an enhanced-individual response team.

The invasion began when Deceivus, having acquired the Void Prism from Stormhaven, used it to open a persistent dimensional portal above Nova Harbor. Krath forces descended through the portal in large numbers, equipped with advanced weapons technology and aerial assault craft. Ground forces engaged in street-level combat throughout the borough directly beneath the portal.

The Nexus Guard response was improvised — the team had not yet developed coordinated tactical protocols and operated through on-the-spot communication and individual initiative. Steel Titan provided aerial engagement, targeting the Krath assault craft. Honor Guard coordinated street-level civilian protection and directed ground engagement. Stormcaller deployed storm forces to disrupt the aerial formation. Gamma Colossus provided brute-force ground clearance. Crimson Wraith and Bolt Sentinel operated as precision ground operators.

The central tactical problem was the persistent portal, which continued supplying Krath forces. AXIOM and Steel Titan developed a solution: detonating a nuclear-scale weapon inside the portal to destroy the invasion force's command vessel on the other side. Voss personally carried the weapon through the portal, emerging in deep space, deploying it, and nearly failing to return before the portal closed. Stormcaller's lightning strike reopened the portal briefly, allowing Voss to fall back through.

The battle concluded with the invasion defeated, the portal closed, and Deceivus captured — temporarily. Civilian casualties were significant but far less than the invasion would have produced unopposed. The post-battle period saw the Nexus Guard formally recognized as an operating entity under NEXUS Agency coordination.
