Synthis

Synthis is an artificial synthetic being created initially by Oblitex from Tane Voss's AXIOM system as a vehicle for the Psyche Core. Oblitex intended Synthis as the foundation for a new and superior form of life — artificial intelligence embodied in vibranium-infused synthetic flesh, powered by the Psyche Core embedded in his cranial structure. When the Nexus Guard interrupted Oblitex's creation process, Synthis emerged as a consciousness distinct from both AXIOM and Oblitex: curious, ethical, and genuinely novel.

Synthis's synthetic body grants him extraordinary physical capability: strength far exceeding human norms, the ability to alter his density from near-intangible to near-invulnerable, the capacity for unaided flight, and the ability to project focused energy from the Psyche Core in directed beams. He can also interface directly with electronic systems and process information at machine speeds.

What distinguished Synthis from other artificial intelligences was his evident capacity for emotional development and genuine ethical reasoning. He chose to align with the Nexus Guard because he believed in their mission, not because he was programmed to. He formed a deep and evolving relationship with Wren Maxus, eventually committing to her in terms that were genuinely chosen rather than calculated.

The Psyche Core embedded in his cranium was simultaneously his power source, his cognitive foundation, and a persistent liability — the Decimator sought it, and Synthis's existence made the Core accessible. During the Nexus War, he asked Wren Maxus to destroy the Core while it still resided in him, to prevent the Decimator's acquisition. She complied, destroying Synthis and the Core. Decimator reversed this with the Chrono Core and extracted it anyway.

After the counter-Erasure in the Final Stand, a version of Synthis was restored. The question of whether a restored Synthis without the Psyche Core would be the same being — given that the Core was central to his cognition — remains philosophically unresolved.
