Honor Guard

Honor Guard is the heroic identity of Sven Rogan, a supersoldier created during the global conflict known as the Second Dominion War. Rogan was a physically weak but extraordinarily determined young man from Nova Harbor who volunteered for an experimental biological enhancement program. Injected with a compound called the Apex Serum, Rogan's physiology was elevated to the peak of human capability: maximum muscle density, accelerated cellular regeneration, heightened reflexes, and greatly enhanced endurance.

Rogan's primary weapon and defensive tool is the Honor Disc — a circular shield constructed from a nearly indestructible Aurite-steel composite. The disc can be thrown with precision to strike multiple targets and returns to Rogan's hand via controlled spin dynamics. It also absorbs kinetic impacts, providing exceptional protection in close-quarters combat.

During the Second Dominion War, Honor Guard fought Vortex Syndicate forces across multiple theaters. He confronted Null Mask, the Vortex Syndicate's leader, aboard a weapons transport called the Valkyrie and crashed the vessel into the polar ice to prevent a mass weapon deployment. Rogan remained frozen in the ice for nearly seventy years until NEXUS Agency recovered and revived him.

Awakening in the modern era disoriented Rogan but did not diminish his values or determination. He joined the Nexus Guard under Director Dax Frey's leadership and formed particularly strong bonds with Tane Voss and Sky Sentinel. During the Nexus Schism, Rogan opposed the Kelvoria Accords, believing that the Nexus Guard must remain free of political control to act decisively. This placed him in direct conflict with Steel Titan and fractured the team.

Following the Erasure, Rogan served as an anchor for the remaining Nexus Guard. After the Final Stand and the counter-Erasure, Rogan used the Chrono Core functionality to return the original Honor Disc to an earlier point in time — and chose to remain in the past, living out his life with a long-lost partner rather than returning to the present. An aged Rogan later passed the Honor Disc to his successor.
