Final Stand

The Final Stand was the culminating conflict of the Nexus War era, fought five years after the Erasure, in which the surviving Nexus Guard used time travel to retrieve earlier versions of the Nexus Cores and execute a counter-Erasure restoring all those lost.

The plan originated when Micro Warrior and Gamma Colossus discovered that traversal through the Micro Realm — a subquantum dimensional layer — permitted time displacement without altering the current timeline, because each point of departure created a branching alternate timeline. This meant historical events could not be erased, but items from earlier timepoints could be retrieved and brought forward.

The surviving Nexus Guard members divided into small teams, each assigned a specific Nexus Core and a specific historical moment to retrieve it. The operation was called the Chrono Raid.

Teams recovered the Force Core from historical Vexor, the Void Core from the Void Prism in Stormhaven at an earlier period, the Flux Core from its vault, the Psyche Core before its integration into Synthis, and the Chrono Core from Lumis Academy. The Spirit Core's retrieval cost Crimson Wraith her life at the Spirit Core site.

Back in the present, Valen-Prime wielded the assembled replica Nexus Gauntlet and executed the counter-Erasure, restoring all those lost. The exertion destroyed his right arm.

A past-timeline version of the Decimator discovered the plan through captured future-version technology. He transported his entire army forward to the present using a temporal bridge. The Final Stand battle involved the full surviving Nexus Guard plus all restored members, along with forces from Stormhaven, Auroria, Lumis Academy, and many other allies.

Steel Titan ultimately wielded a second Nexus Gauntlet bearing the Nexus Cores and performed a targeted Erasure specifically eliminating the Decimator's entire army. The exertion killed Voss. In the aftermath, surviving members dispersed. Stormcaller departed with a rebuilt Cosmos Patrol. Sven Rogan used the Chrono Core return to travel to the past and live out his life with his partner, leaving the Honor Disc to his successor.
