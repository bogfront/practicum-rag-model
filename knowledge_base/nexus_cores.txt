Nexus Cores

The Nexus Cores are six singular artifacts of incomprehensible power, each containing and controlling a fundamental aspect of existence. They predate the current universe and were among the first objects to exist following the universal formation event. Together they form the complete set that can be concentrated in the Nexus Gauntlet to achieve reality-scale effects. Individually, each Core grants extraordinary power over its specific domain.

Force Core: Controls physical energy and power in all its forms. Possessing the Force Core grants the user the ability to project and manipulate energy at cosmic scales, destroy planetary bodies, and amplify their own physical capability beyond any natural limit. Color signature: violet. Last known location before the Nexus War: the planet Vexor, held by Vexor Corps.

Void Core: Controls space, distance, and dimensional traversal. Possessing the Void Core allows instantaneous relocation of matter across any distance, creation of spatial fields and barriers, and dimensional gate construction. Color signature: blue. Originally housed in the Void Prism artifact, used by Allfather Vorn, later taken by Decimator.

Flux Core: Controls matter and physical reality at the atomic and subatomic level. Possessing the Flux Core allows rewriting of physical matter, altering environments, creating any physical substance, and undoing physical changes. Color signature: red. Last known location: a hidden vault on the planet Svartalheim, encountered by Stormcaller.

Psyche Core: Controls consciousness, emotion, and mental processes. Possessing the Psyche Core enables complete domination of other minds, reading and alteration of memory, and induction of any mental state. Color signature: yellow. Embedded in Synthis's cranial chassis by Decimator during the Oblitex project.

Chrono Core: Controls time, enabling forward and backward traversal, probability manipulation, and observation of alternate timelines. Possessing the Chrono Core allows selective alteration of temporal events within precise constraints. Color signature: green. Held at Lumis Academy within the Chrono Eye, entrusted to the Arcane Weaver.

Spirit Core: Controls the fundamental life-essence of beings. Possessing the Spirit Core enables the manipulation of life force — creation, withdrawal, and transfer — at universal scale. It is guarded by a spiritual entity at a remote planetoid; acquisition requires the sacrifice of something — someone — deeply loved. Color signature: orange.
