The Erasure

The Erasure is the term used for the universe-scale event executed by the Decimator upon completing the Nexus Gauntlet with all six Nexus Cores. With a single activation gesture, the Decimator randomly eliminated exactly half of all living beings across the entire universe simultaneously. The dissolution was instantaneous and painless from the experiential perspective of those affected — individuals simply ceased to exist mid-action, mid-sentence, mid-thought, dissolving into fine particles.

The Erasure's effects were immediately apparent globally and across space. Vehicles operated by eliminated drivers crashed. Aircraft fell. Medical procedures were interrupted when surgeons disappeared. Children lost parents and vice versa with no pattern or logic other than statistical randomness. The psychological impact on survivors was immense and lasted years.

On the planetary scale, the event produced a period of acute global crisis followed by a protracted societal adjustment. Emergency services were overwhelmed. Infrastructure failed in multiple locations. Supply chains, dependent on eliminated personnel at every level, collapsed in many regions. The following years required intensive reconstruction of social, economic, and institutional systems with half the workforce and population.

The Nexus Guard survivors — Steel Titan, Honor Guard, Stormcaller (among the restored, as an earlier timeline version participated in the Final Stand), Gamma Colossus, Bolt Sentinel, Crimson Wraith, Dax Frey (erased), Stellar Guard (erased), and others — spent five years grieving, sustaining what systems they could, and eventually pursuing the Chrono Raid.

Following the counter-Erasure during the Final Stand, those eliminated by the Erasure were restored. They returned to the present moment — five years later than they had left — with no subjective experience of the intervening time. The social and psychological consequences of this sudden restoration, combined with the changes in the world during the five years, represented a significant ongoing challenge for global society in the years after the Final Stand.
